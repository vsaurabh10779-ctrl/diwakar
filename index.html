About me

I'm a frontend developer who cares about how things feel as much as how they work.

Most of my time goes into component architecture, design systems and the unglamorous performance work — bundle budgets, layout shift, keyboard navigation. I'd rather ship one thing properly than five things halfway.

I work mostly in React and TypeScript, style with modern CSS (and Tailwind when a team needs it), and treat accessibility and Lighthouse scores as features — not cleanups for later. Right now I'm open to freelance work and full-time roles.
